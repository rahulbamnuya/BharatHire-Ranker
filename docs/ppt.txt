SLIDE 2: THE RECRUITMENT BOTTLENECK: SCALE, TRAPS & COMPUTE COSTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Slide Title]
The Recruitment Bottleneck: Scale, Traps & Compute Costs

[On-Slide Points]
* The Scale Challenge
  Processing 100,000+ candidates for a single role overwhelms memory and throughput in traditional screening pipelines.
* The "Honeypot" Threat
  Spam profiles with inconsistent timelines and keyword stuffing evade simple keyword or frequency-based filters.
* High API & Infra Costs
  Relying on commercial LLM APIs at scale is cost-prohibitive and adds network latency for thousands of candidate screens.
* Lack of Explainability
  Black-box rankings leave recruiters without fact-based, auditable reasons for candidate prioritisation.

[Speaker Notes]
Hiring teams face a massive bottleneck when filtering candidate databases. Standard setups crash on 100k dumps, fail to detect chronological fraud (honeypots), generate massive API bills, and output black-box ranking scores without explainable reasons.

Our Solution: BharatHire AI Ranker

SLIDE 4: 3-STAGE PIPELINE DESIGN
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Slide Title]
3-Stage Pipeline Design

[On-Slide Flowchart / Phases]
1. Filter (CPU Heuristics)
2. Embed (Local MiniLM)
3. Scale (23-Signal Rank)

[Summary Description]
Each stage is tuned for computational efficiency and recruiter relevance: fast heuristic culling, local semantic scoring, then multi-signal behavioural ranking.

[Speaker Notes]
The pipeline is structured as a funnel. First, CPU heuristics filter out unqualified and inconsistent profiles. Second, a local embedding model calculates semantic job description match. Finally, the candidates are scored and scaled against 23 engagement signals to produce the top 100 list.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 5: STAGE DETAILS — STAGE 1: FILTER (CPU HEURISTICS)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Slide Title]
Stage Details — Stage 1: Filter (CPU Heuristics)

[On-Slide Details]
* 01 | Honeypot Removal
  Chronology checks, founder/company date cross-validation, and keyword density heuristics to drop fraudulent profiles early.
* 02 | Title & Experience Filter
  Reject unrelated titles and candidates with <3 years relevant experience to meet role minima.
* 03 | Fast I/O Streaming
  Memory-efficient JSONL streaming prevents OOM (Out-of-Memory) when parsing massive candidate dumps.

[Speaker Notes]
In Stage 1, we parse candidates line-by-line to prevent memory leaks. We execute timeline audits, checking for start/end conflicts, overlapping roles, and founding date violations (such as claiming a role at Sarvam AI before 2023).

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 6: STAGE DETAILS — STAGE 2: EMBED (LOCAL SEMANTIC SEARCH)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Slide Title]
Stage Details — Stage 2: Embed (Local Semantic Search)

[On-Slide Description]
We run all-MiniLM-L6-v2 locally on CPU to compute sentence embeddings and cosine similarity against the job description. This provides strong semantic recall while remaining CPU-feasible.

[On-Slide Bullet Points]
* Batching and quantised matrix ops for throughput
* Cosine thresholds tuned to balance precision and recall
* Fallback lexical checks for narrow technical tokens

[Speaker Notes]
Stage 2 introduces semantic matching. Rather than relying on simple keyword checking, we run the all-MiniLM model offline. We compare the combined text profile against the Job Description to ensure deep ML/AI systems experience is captured.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 7: STAGE DETAILS — STAGE 3: SCALE (BEHAVIOURAL SCORING)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Slide Title]
Stage Details — Stage 3: Scale (Behavioural Scoring)

[On-Slide Description]
Final ranking combines semantic match with 23 Redrob platform signals to surface candidates who are both relevant and likely to convert.

[On-Slide Grid]
* Activity Signals
  GitHub commits, repo recency, and open-source contribution trends.
* Availability & Notice
  Notice period, current employment status and recruiter response history.
* Engagement & Quality
  Profile completeness, endorsement counts and prior interview attendance.

[Speaker Notes]
A candidate who is technically perfect but unreachable is a hiring failure. Stage 3 integrates 23 platform signals, rewarding active, responsive, verified candidates with short notice periods while penalizing ghosters.

SLIDE 8: KEY TECHNICAL INNOVATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Slide Title]
Key Technical Innovations

[On-Slide List]
* 1 | Chronological Auditing
  Detects overlapping roles, impossible start dates and other timeline anomalies to neutralise honeypots.
* 2 | Decimal-Safe Sorting
  Rounds scores for validator compliance and ties are resolved deterministically by candidate ID ordering.
* 3 | Interactive Tuning UI
  Streamlit frontend with sliders for Years-of-Experience, skill weight and notice period to customise live rankings.
* 4 | Zero Memory Leaks
  Streaming parsers and chunked processing ensure safe operation on large JSONL files without OOMs.

[Speaker Notes]
Our key engineering highlights include: strict timeline auditing to reject honeypots, rounded scoring and determinism to guarantee validation success, and an interactive UI allowing live weight-tuning.

Roadmap & Next Steps
